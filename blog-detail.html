How Online Booking Reduces Missed Appointments

By Lina Haddad | 20 October 2026 | Category: Appointments

Missed appointments cost clinics time and money, and they leave other patients waiting longer for a free slot.

Why patients miss appointments

Most missed visits are not intentional. Patients forget the date, cannot reach the clinic by phone to cancel, or are unsure of the exact time.

What online booking changes

When patients choose their own time, they are more likely to remember it. Automatic reminders add a second layer of protection.

Our no-show rate dropped noticeably once patients started receiving reminders.

Getting started

A clinic can begin with a simple booking page and reminders, then add more features as it grows. You can contact us to discuss the right plan.
