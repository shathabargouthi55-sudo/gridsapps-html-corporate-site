Online Appointment Booking

A simple booking system that lets patients choose their own appointment time, any hour of the day.

What you get

  • A booking page patients can open from any phone
  • Automatic reminders before each appointment
  • A daily schedule view for your reception team
  • Easy rescheduling and cancellation

How it works for patients

  1. The patient opens the booking page.
  2. They choose a doctor and an available time.
  3. They receive a confirmation and a reminder.
