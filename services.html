Health Tech Glossary

Common terms you will hear when working with digital health systems.

EHR
Electronic Health Record: a digital version of a patient's medical history.
Telemedicine
Providing medical consultations remotely through video or phone.
Interoperability
The ability of different health systems to exchange and use data.
